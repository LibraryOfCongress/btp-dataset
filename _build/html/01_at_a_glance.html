The By the People Comprehensive Transcription Data Package provides access to Library of Congress digital pages transcribed by public volunteers, through the Library of Congress’s By the People crowdsourced transcription program. The dataset combines full text transcripts from contributors, tags from contributors, selected metadata fields from Library of Congress staff, and URL pointers to download images as IIIF JPEG, JP2, and/or TIFF. Read more about the data at About the Data. Info about downloading the data is at Get the Data.

Image#

The dataset includes pointers to images in the following formats (when available):

  • IIIF JPEG (embedded here at 12.5% size)

  • JP2 (JPEG2000)

  • TIFF

Note

Occassionally, an image may not be available for a page, or may only be available in certain formats.
