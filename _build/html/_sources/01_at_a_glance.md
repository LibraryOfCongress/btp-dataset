The **By the People Comprehensive Transcription Data Package** provides access to Library of Congress digital pages transcribed by public volunteers, through the Library of Congress’s [By the People](https://crowd.loc.gov/) crowdsourced transcription program. The dataset combines full text transcripts from contributors, tags from contributors, selected metadata fields from Library of Congress staff, and URL pointers to download images as [IIIF](https://www.loc.gov/apis/micro-services/image-services/) JPEG, JP2, and/or TIFF. Read more about the data at {doc}`02_about`. Info about downloading the data is at {doc}`03_download`.
